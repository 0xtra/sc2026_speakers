Conference Tips

Practical notes to get the most out of your time on the ground.

🎙️ For the 5-min podcast clips

Set up a quiet spot or backstage corner. Ask one tight question to start — “If a Mexican business owner asked you one thing about stablecoins, what would you say?” gets the best answers. Keep it conversational, not a pitch.

📝 For thought leadership

Pre-schedule these before the day gets chaotic. Patrick Witt (White House) and Katrina Paglia (Pantera) will be in heavy demand — email their teams early. Plan 20–30 min and prepare a clear thesis.

📱 For short-form video

The best clips come from hallway moments right after panels, when speakers are still “warmed up.” Lead with a spiky hook: “What’s the most wrong thing people believe about stablecoins?”

🇲🇽 Lean into the local angle

Your audience cares about Mexico. Whenever possible, steer global speakers toward Mexico-specific examples. Mexican voices (Vogel, Murat, Martinez, Jacob Levin) should anchor your most shareable content.

🤝 Have a fallback list ready

Speakers cancel and schedules shift. Keep 2–3 Tier 2 alternates per slot. If a regulator falls through, Caitlin Barnett (Chainalysis) is a strong swap for Paglia.

📋 Prep one universal question

Have one great question you ask everyone — e.g. “What’s one thing a small business should do this year to get ready for digital money?” It creates a thread you can cut across all your content.
